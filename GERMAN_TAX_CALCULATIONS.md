## Calculation Methodology

### 1. German Tax Framework (Abgeltungssteuer)

**Gold:**
- Tax Rate: **0%** (tax-free after 1 year holding period)
- Legal Basis: Spekulationssteuer-Ausnahmeregelung
- Application: 100% of gains exempt from taxation

**Stocks (SMIC, S&P 500, DAX):**
- Base Capital Gains Tax: 25% (Abgeltungssteuer)
- Solidarity Surcharge: 5.5% (on the 25%)
- **Total Tax Rate: 26.375%**
- Application: On all capital gains, regardless of holding period

### 2. Formulas Used

#### Effective Annual Return (After Tax)
```
Effective Annual Return = Gross Annual Return × (1 - Tax Rate)
```

Examples:
- Gold: 10.40% × (1 - 0.00) = **10.40%**
- SMIC: 7.90% × (1 - 0.26375) = 7.90% × 0.73625 = **5.82%**
- S&P 500: 9.10% × (1 - 0.26375) = 9.10% × 0.73625 = **6.70%**
- DAX: 3.80% × (1 - 0.26375) = 3.80% × 0.73625 = **2.80%**

#### Final Value with 25-Year Compounding
```
Final Value = Initial Investment × (1 + Effective Annual Rate)^25
Final Value = 10,000€ × (1 + r)^25
```

Examples:
- Gold: 10,000€ × (1.1040)^25 = **126,596€**
- SMIC: 10,000€ × (1.0582)^25 = **68,311€**
- S&P 500: 10,000€ × (1.0670)^25 = **59,694€**
- DAX: 10,000€ × (1.0280)^25 = **20,352€**

#### Total Tax Impact
```
Total Tax = Total Gain × Tax Rate
```

Where: Total Gain = Gross Final Value - Initial Investment (10,000€)

Examples:
- Gold: 116,596€ × 0.00 = **0€**
- SMIC: 79,200€ × 0.26375 = **20,889€**
- S&P 500: 67,496€ × 0.26375 = **17,802€**
- DAX: 14,060€ × 0.26375 = **3,708€**

#### Net After-Tax Value
```
Net Value = Gross Final Value - Total Tax
```

Examples:
- Gold: 126,596€ - 0€ = **126,596€**
- SMIC: 89,200€ - 20,889€ = **68,311€**
- S&P 500: 77,496€ - 17,802€ = **59,694€**
- DAX: 24,060€ - 3,708€ = **20,352€**

German tax treatment applies taxes annually on capital gains, creating a compounding drag effect:

### The Tax Drag Effect

Without tax:
- 10,000€ × (1.091)^25 = **88,231€**

With 26.375% tax:
- 10,000€ × (1.0670)^25 = **59,694€**

**Tax reduction factor: 32.3%** (28,537€ lost to compounding effects)

Each year, the government takes 26.375% of gains, leaving less capital to grow in subsequent years. This "drag" compounds dramatically over 25 years.

## Why Annual Taxation Matters

Unlike one-time taxation of final gains, German annual capital gains taxation compounds negatively:

1. **Year 1:** Investor gains 910€, pays 240€ tax, has 10,670€ to invest
2. **Year 2:** Growth is calculated on 10,670€, not original 10,000€ + full 910€ gain
3. **Year 3-25:** This "tax drag" compounds, creating exponentially lower final values

Over 25 years, this effect reduces stock final values by approximately 32% compared to tax-free investing.
